# Code of conduct

Short, because it is not complicated.

## The rule

Be decent to people. Argue with the work, not the person doing it.

This project is about machines being reliably present for strangers. It would be
odd to build that and be unpleasant to the strangers building it.

## What that means here

- **Assume the other person is trying.** Most bad patches and confused issues
  come from someone who cared enough to try, on hardware you do not have.
- **Say what is wrong, and how you know.** "This is wrong" helps nobody. "This
  is wrong, here is the measurement" is a contribution.
- **Being right is not a licence to be unkind.** It is easy to be correct and
  useless at the same time.
- **Nobody owes you a review, a merge, or a reply.** Maintainers are people with
  jobs.

## Not welcome

Harassment, personal attacks, or abuse of any kind. Discrimination on race, sex,
gender identity, sexuality, disability, appearance, age, religion, nationality
or anything else in that family. Sexual attention where it was not invited.
Publishing someone's private information. Sustained disruption after being asked
to stop.

Also not welcome, because it is specific to this kind of project: telling people
a home node earns money. It does not, we say so plainly, and posts implying
otherwise put real people at real financial risk.

## Scope

Anywhere this project happens: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, and any space where you are representing easyNode.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer. Contact details are in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), which
is the same route as a vulnerability report and is read with the same
seriousness.

Reports are handled privately. You will not be asked to argue your case in a
public thread. Consequences range from a request to stop, through edited or
removed content, to a permanent ban, and the maintainer decides.

If the person you need to report is the maintainer, say so in the subject line
and say it plainly. That is a fair thing to raise and it will not be held
against you.
